# Pre-registration 08_maxlen_128_vs_256: Maximum length 128 vs 256 at inference

| field | content |
|---|---|
| Hypothesis | H: reading 256 instead of 128 tokens at inference does not change P@1 on the 34 test sentences longer than 128 RoBERTa tokens by more than 3 sentences. |
| Split | test, the 34 truncated sentences (ids in _evidence_pack/01_truncated_test_ids.csv) |
| Metric | P@1 on those sentences |
| Comparison | roberta_pw_s42 and secbert_pw_s42 with max_length 128 vs 256 (inference only; the models were TRAINED at 128) |
| Seeds | 42 |
| Dev-only tuning | nothing tuned |
| Decision rule | Report both; exact McNemar on the 34. |
| What counts as failure / prior exposure | Models were trained at 128, so 256 at inference is out of distribution; a null or negative result does not show that training at 256 would not help (that needs retraining: notebook 03, TODO-COLAB). |

Split discipline: anything tuned is tuned on DEV only. The test split is evaluated once per configuration listed here. Uncertainty: paired/percentile bootstrap over test sentences, 10,000 resamples, numpy default_rng(20261114). Systems (fixed): SVM (refit, deterministic); RoBERTa and SecBERT = mean probability of seeds 42-44; matcher = mean score of seeds 42-44; fusion = z-score sum with weights (3,1,1,1) in the order matcher, SVM, RoBERTa, SecBERT.
